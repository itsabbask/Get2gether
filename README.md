# The Get2Gether Hospitality Group: website UX/UI draft

Static, responsive draft of theget2gether.com built from the approved website copy (October 6, 2026).

## Pages
- `index.html` Home
- `about.html` About
- `services.html` Services (Catering, Private Dining, Corporate & Brand Events)
- `experiences.html` Experiences (Supper Clubs, Pop-Ups, Seasonal Specials)
- `menus.html` Menus
- `gallery.html` Gallery with category filter
- `faq.html` FAQ accordion
- `inquire.html` 4 step inquiry form (front end only, no submission backend yet)

## Notes
- Photos in `images/` are free stock photos from Unsplash used as placeholders. Replace with real dish and event photography before launch.
- Logo is a text placeholder until the approved brand kit is supplied.
- Co-founder portraits are placeholders.
- The inquiry form needs a form handler (delivery to Info@TheGet2Gether.com, spam protection, confirmation email) and service preselection from page buttons.
- Fonts: Fraunces and Manrope via Google Fonts.
