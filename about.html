Welcome to The Get2Gether

Good food brings us together.

The Get2Gether Hospitality Group was created around a simple idea: gathering should feel meaningful, welcoming, and full of flavor. We believe the food matters, and so does the way people feel when they sit down to enjoy it.

Our approach blends comforting favorites, vibrant flavors, thoughtful presentation, and the warmth of Southern hospitality. Whether we are preparing a shared meal, a private dinner, or a curated experience, our goal is to make the occasion feel personal.

Meet Dawn Owens-Vaughn

“For me, cooking has always been a way to care for people.”

I love the process of building a menu, preparing a dish with intention, and watching a gathering come to life around the table.

Charles and I created The Get2Gether to share that feeling beyond my own home. My food is inspired by the comfort of Southern cooking, the bold flavors I love, and the joy of exploring dishes from different cultures. I want every experience to feel thoughtful, flavorful, and welcoming.

Before building The Get2Gether, I spent years leading client relationships in the technology industry. That experience shaped the way I listen, plan, and pay attention to the details. Today, I bring that same care to the people who trust me with their gatherings.

Whether you are celebrating something special or simply making time for good company, I would love to help you create a meal worth gathering around.
