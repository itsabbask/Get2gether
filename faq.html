FAQ

A few details before we gather.

Helpful answers about our services, planning, and booking. Every event has its own details. These answers are a starting point; your proposal and agreement will confirm the terms for your gathering.

Catering

What catering options do you offer?

We offer pickup, drop-off, and staffed catering options, subject to availability and your event requirements. Your proposal will confirm the menu, service style, and included items.

Can I customize my menu?

Yes. Sample menus are a starting point. Share your favorite flavors, occasion, and preferences so we can discuss the right menu for your guests.

Is there a minimum order or guest count?

Minimums depend on the menu and service style. We will confirm any required minimum with your quote.

Are staffing, setup, and rentals included?

Only the items listed in your proposal are included. Let us know if you need staffing, setup, serving equipment, tableware, rentals, or cleanup so we can discuss those details.

Where do you provide service?

Include your city, ZIP code, and venue in your inquiry. We will confirm whether we can accommodate the location and whether delivery or travel charges apply.

Can you accommodate allergies or dietary preferences?

Please share all allergies and restrictions before menu approval. We will confirm which requests we can accommodate. Food may be prepared in a kitchen that handles common allergens, so we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

Private Dining & Experiences

What is included in a private dinner?

Each dinner is planned individually. Your proposal will confirm the courses, food preparation, plating, service, and any agreed additions. Decor, rentals, and tablescape coordination are included only when specified.

Can you cook or serve at my home or venue?

Share your location, available kitchen facilities, and any venue restrictions. We will review access, preparation, and service needs before confirming the event.

How do I attend a supper club or pop-up?

Published events will have their own reservation or ticket link. Review the listing for the menu, location, timing, inclusions, dietary options, and event-specific terms.

Are beverages or cocktails included?

Beverage inclusions vary by event and will be clearly stated. Any alcohol service requires a separately confirmed arrangement with an appropriate provider.

Corporate & Brand Events

What types of business events can you support?

We welcome inquiries for client appreciation, executive gatherings, team celebrations, launches, brand activations, and networking occasions. Share your objectives so we can discuss the best fit.

Can you provide a proposal for internal approval?

Yes. We can outline the proposed menu, service scope, and pricing for review. Include your billing contact, approval process, and any purchasing requirements in your inquiry.

Can the menu reflect our event theme?

We can discuss menu choices and presentation that complement your occasion. Any specialty styling or brand-specific requests will be confirmed in the proposal.

Booking, Payments & Policies

How do I begin?

Select Plan Your Get2Gether and complete the inquiry form. We will review your event details and availability, then follow up with questions or next steps.

Does submitting an inquiry reserve my date?

No. A preferred date or inquiry does not reserve event capacity. Your event is confirmed only after we accept the booking and the required agreement and payment steps are complete.

Do you require a booking retainer?

Custom events require a booking payment as specified in your proposal and agreement. This payment is credited toward your event total. Your documents will state the amount and payment schedule.

When are the final balance and guest count due?

Your proposal and agreement will list the deadlines for final payment, guest count, menu selections, and service details. Short-notice bookings may require full payment to confirm.

What happens if I cancel or need to change the date?

Contact us as soon as possible. Date changes depend on availability and written approval. Cancellation charges and any refund are determined by your signed agreement, including applicable completed work and committed event costs.

Can I change my menu or guest count?

Requests are subject to availability, the agreed deadlines, and any pricing changes. Changes must be approved in writing before they become part of your booking.

How far ahead should I inquire?

Reach out as soon as you have a date in mind, especially for holidays and busy weekends. We will review short-notice requests when capacity allows.

How are seasonal pickup orders handled?

Fixed packages use their own ordering instructions, pickup windows, payment requirements, and order terms. Follow the details shown on the current offer.
